Verification infrastructure with bounded authority and reproducible evidence.

Proof, not posture

Four constraints define the system.

01

Deterministic CUORE

Critical policy decisions stay machine-checkable instead of becoming model judgment.

02

HumanGate

Human authority remains explicit when scope, policy, risk, submission, signing, or spend requires it.

03

Exact-head verification

Browser and isolated Docker qualification bind evidence to the exact commit under review.

04

Reproducible evidence

Claims are packaged with replayable artifacts instead of confidence scores or decorative metrics.

Safety boundary

What BOQA must not do.

  • expand its own scope
  • scan unauthorized targets
  • treat model output as proof
  • auto-submit sensitive findings
  • sign or spend without explicit authority
