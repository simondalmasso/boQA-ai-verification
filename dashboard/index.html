How it works

A bounded path from proposal to proof.

  1. MODELSuggests work; never grants authority.
  2. CUORENormalizes inputs deterministically.
  3. POLICY / SCOPEConstrains what may proceed.
  4. HUMANGATEReceives ambiguity and sensitive decisions.
  5. EXECUTERuns only within the accepted boundary.
  6. VERIFYQualifies behavior against exact revisions.
  7. EVIDENCERecords what was actually proven.

Safety boundary

BOQA cannot:

  • expand its own scope
  • scan unauthorized targets
  • treat model output as proof
  • auto-submit sensitive findings
  • sign or spend without explicit authority
